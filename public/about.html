ABOUT MILKIBA

A Simple Way to Manage Milk Transactions

MILKIBA is a digital milk marketplace designed to connect vendors, sellers and buyers in one organized platform. It helps manage daily milk collection, supply, sales, quantities, rates, payments and transaction history.

What is MILKIBA?

A centralized platform for managing the complete milk transaction process.

Built for a connected milk marketplace

Traditional milk transactions can involve handwritten records, manual calculations, repeated communication and difficulty tracking previous transactions.

MILKIBA provides a digital platform where the different participants can work together while maintaining their own responsibilities.

The system is organized around three main roles: Vendor, Seller and Buyer. Each role has its own dashboard and functions.

What MILKIBA manages

1
Milk Quantity

Record the quantity collected, supplied or sold.

2
Milk Rate

Record the applicable rate for each transaction.

3
Payments

Maintain payment-related transaction information.

4
History

Keep date-wise transaction records for future reference.

Three Roles. One Platform.

MILKIBA separates responsibilities while keeping the complete transaction flow connected.

V

Vendor

The Vendor manages the operational side of the marketplace and records the actual milk transactions.

  • Manage buyers and sellers
  • Record collected milk
  • Set transaction rates
  • Manage milk supply and sales
  • Correct previous records
S

Seller

Sellers provide milk to the Vendor and can view their transaction information through their dashboard.

  • View milk collection records
  • View applicable rates
  • Track transaction history
  • Receive relevant notifications
  • Respond to record changes
B

Buyer

Buyers purchase available milk and can view their transaction details and history.

  • View milk purchase records
  • View quantity and rate
  • Track payment information
  • Receive relevant notifications
  • Review transaction history

How MILKIBA Works

The complete transaction process can be organized into a simple sequence.

1

Collection

Vendor records the milk collected from the seller.

2

Quantity

The actual milk quantity is recorded.

3

Rate

The applicable rate is recorded for the transaction.

4

Sale

Milk can be supplied or sold to the buyer.

5

History

The transaction remains available for future reference.

Morning & Evening Sessions

MILKIBA can organize daily milk operations into separate sessions.

Morning Session

The morning session can be used to record the first milk collection, supply and transaction activities of the day.

  • Seller milk collection
  • Quantity recording
  • Rate entry
  • Buyer supply
  • Payment information

Evening Session

The evening session provides a separate record for the second operational period of the day.

  • Evening milk collection
  • Quantity recording
  • Rate entry
  • Buyer supply
  • Payment information

Organized Transaction Records

Important transaction information can be maintained in a structured way.

Quantity

Record the actual quantity of milk collected or supplied.

Rate

Record the rate associated with the particular transaction.

Total Amount

Transaction amount can be calculated using quantity × applicable rate.

Date & Session

Records can be associated with a specific date and morning/evening session.

Account & Data Management

MILKIBA separates user access according to account roles and responsibilities.

Controlled Access

Users access the platform using their registered accounts. Different roles receive different dashboards and responsibilities.

Authentication

Users authenticate through their registered MILKIBA accounts.

Role-Based Access

Buyer, Seller and Vendor accounts are handled separately.

Admin Management

Administrative functions are separated from normal user accounts.

MILKIBA Administration

The Admin area is designed for authorized administrative operations such as reviewing password reset requests and managing administrative actions.

Admin access is restricted to the authorized administrator account.
